Reputation

On reputation laundering

Some companies are not selling products. They are buying cultural acceptability. Fashion drops, pop-ups, and tasteful objects can become reputation shields when the underlying business is far harder to defend.

AI

AI is an intelligence layer, not a thinking layer

The real value of AI in communications is pattern recognition, audit, and optimisation. The second a team mistakes speed for judgement, the output becomes faster, cleaner, and less trustworthy.

Culture

Loaded words change how messages land

Working on Islamic art communications taught me that audiences do not encounter neutral language. They bring politics, memory, identity, and instinct into the room before the story even begins.
